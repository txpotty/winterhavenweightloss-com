    What Causes Low Libido in Horizon West

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Common contributors include SSRIs and SNRIs, hormonal birth control, perimenopause, thyroid problems, chronic stress, poor sleep, pain during sex and relationship strain. So does how you feel in your body. When low desire is persistent, distressing and not better explained by another cause, it may be diagnosed as hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. Women whose desire faded after a new medication, a baby, perimenopause or a hard year often benefit. You can start without a label. If lower desire does not bother you, there is nothing to fix, because distress is part of the definition. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Horizon West

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is a compounded option. It is used off-label as a spray or troche before intimacy and is not FDA-approved. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Early steps often include adjusting a medication, treating dryness or improving sleep, sometimes alongside a new prescription. PT-141 effects show up within hours. Flibanserin takes four to eight weeks, and it is typically stopped at the eight week mark if there is no benefit. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Horizon West

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Alcohol and certain interacting drugs make them stronger, which is why it is taken at bedtime with drinking limits. Nausea is common with PT-141. It can also cause flushing, headache, injection site reactions, a brief rise in blood pressure and darker skin patches with repeated use. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. It is also avoided with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as fluconazole, clarithromycin or some HIV medications, and alcohol must be limited near bedtime. Blood pressure matters for PT-141. Women with high blood pressure that is not controlled, or with heart disease, should not use PT-141. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Horizon West

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. Some women prefer that, though nausea can be a drawback. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
