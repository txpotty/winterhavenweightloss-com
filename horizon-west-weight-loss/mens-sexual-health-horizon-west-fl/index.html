    How ED Medication Works in Horizon West

    Most ED treatment uses PDE5 inhibitors, a drug class that blocks the enzyme which shuts down blood flow into the penis after arousal, so the erection is easier to get and keep. Without sexual stimulation, nothing happens. Arousal is still required. Viagra contains sildenafil, Cialis contains tadalafil, and both are sold as FDA-approved generics.

    Who Men's Sexual Health Care Is For

    This care is for adult men who have trouble getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex, or whose interest in sex has dropped in a way that bothers them. Risk rises with age and with diabetes or high blood pressure. Stress counts too. Poor sleep and heavy drinking play a role for many men, and some blood pressure or antidepressant medications can cause it.

    Sildenafil for As-Needed Use in Horizon West

    Sildenafil is usually taken 30 to 60 minutes before sex, comes in 25, 50 and 100 mg tablets, and works for about 4 to 6 hours after a dose. A heavy, high-fat meal can delay it by an hour. Empty stomach works best. Your provider picks the starting strength and changes it based on results, with no more than 1 dose in 24 hours.

    Tadalafil, As Needed or Daily

    Tadalafil taken as needed, usually 10 or 20 mg, can work for up to 36 hours, which is why some people call it the weekend pill and prefer it for less planning. A low daily dose of 2.5 or 5 mg removes the need to plan. Food barely affects it. Daily tadalafil is also FDA-approved for urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate.

    Chewables, Dissolving Tablets and Troches in Horizon West

    Daily ED chewables, rapid dissolve tablets and troches are compounded forms of sildenafil or tadalafil that melt in the mouth or under the tongue instead of being swallowed with water. Some men simply prefer not to swallow pills. These are not FDA-approved. They come from licensed US compounding pharmacies, and dosing varies by formula.

    PT-141 for Low Desire

    PT-141, or bremelanotide, works on melanocortin receptors in the brain and is aimed at desire rather than blood flow, which makes it a different tool from sildenafil or tadalafil. Use in men is off-label. Nausea is common. It also raises blood pressure briefly, so heart screening matters even more, and some men use it alongside a PDE5 inhibitor.

    Side Effects and Safety in Horizon West

    Common side effects of ED pills include headache, facial flushing, stuffy nose and indigestion, and they usually fade within a few hours as the medication wears off. Tadalafil can cause back or muscle aches. Sildenafil can tint vision blue. Never combine ED pills with nitrates or poppers, because blood pressure can crash, and an erection over 4 hours is an emergency.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The questionnaire asks about chest pain, blood pressure, heart attack or stroke history, liver and kidney disease, and every medication you take, including supplements bought online. Nitrates and riociguat rule out PDE5 inhibitors completely. Alpha blockers need care. A home or pharmacy blood pressure reading from the last 6 months helps.
