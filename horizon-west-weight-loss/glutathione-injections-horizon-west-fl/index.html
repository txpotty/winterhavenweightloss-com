    How Glutathione Works in Horizon West

    Glutathione is made from glutamate, cysteine and glycine. The liver produces the most, and the molecule neutralizes free radicals and regenerates vitamins C and E so they can keep working. Detox pathways depend on it. Levels can fall with age, illness and chronic stress, and injections deliver glutathione directly into tissue, avoiding the gut breakdown that limits most oral forms.

    Who Glutathione Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want antioxidant support or better recovery. People on GLP-1 medication or NAD+ sometimes add glutathione, but it has no effect on body weight and is not a treatment for a diagnosed disease. Skin lightening is excluded. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, glutathione allergy and poorly controlled asthma usually rule it out.

    Glutathione Side Effects in Horizon West

    Injection site pain leads the list. Redness and bruising where you inject are common, along with occasional mild nausea, abdominal cramping, bloating or a temporary headache. Rash or itching can signal an allergy, and because inhaled glutathione has triggered airway tightening in asthma, your provider is especially cautious with breathing history. Wheezing needs immediate care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and breathing health first. Expect questions on pregnancy, asthma control, allergies, organ health and current cancer treatment, which can be affected by antioxidants. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if available.

    IV drips are the usual comparison. An IV push gives more in one sitting at a clinic, and home injections trade that for speed and convenience. Oral capsules are poorly absorbed. N-acetylcysteine, a supplement, provides cysteine so the body can make more of its own glutathione, and NAD+ injections target cellular energy rather than antioxidant defense.
