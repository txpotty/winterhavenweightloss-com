    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Horizon West, FL

    How Zepbound Works in Horizon West

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. GIP and GLP-1 together help regulate appetite, slow how fast the stomach empties and improve the way the body handles blood sugar after a meal. Many notice fewer cravings and smaller portions. You inject it once weekly into the belly, thigh or upper arm.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. It is also approved for adults with a BMI of 27 or higher who have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. In late 2024 the FDA added moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Horizon West

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. The 2.5 mg dose is a starter dose meant to help your body adjust, and it is not where most of the effect comes from. From there, doses climb in 2.5 mg steps every four weeks or more, through 5, 7.5, 10 and 12.5 mg to a top of 15 mg. Most people settle at 5 mg, 10 mg or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Horizon West

    The first month is mostly quiet. You may notice a smaller appetite within the first week, and stomach upset tends to cluster in the day or two after a shot or a dose increase. The SURMOUNT-1 clinical study reported average weight loss of roughly 20 percent at the 15 mg dose over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Vomiting, constipation, indigestion, stomach pain and redness at the injection site are also common, especially during the first days after each dose increase. Rarer and more serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones, dehydration that harms the kidneys and allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Horizon West

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. Anyone with medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in their family is not a candidate, and Zepbound is not used while pregnant. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Horizon West

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. Zepbound carries the weight management label and Mounjaro carries the type 2 diabetes label, which matters for insurance and for how your provider writes the prescription. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide comes from compounding pharmacies and is not FDA-approved.
