    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in St. Cloud, FL

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in St. Cloud

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil works on a separate track, lengthening the growth phase.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. Your photos confirm the pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in St. Cloud

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label daily pill some providers prescribe instead, typically starting around 1.25 to 2.5 mg. Compounded sprays that combine finasteride and minoxidil go on once daily. Dutasteride, when used, is a daily capsule.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. Around months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening. Expect the full effect near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in St. Cloud

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. These usually resolve after stopping, and many men who notice them find they fade even while staying on the medicine. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil sometimes brings body hair or fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Mention it at your next prostate screening.

    How Men's Options Compare in St. Cloud

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Men avoiding DHT blockers can use a compounded minoxidil and GHK-Cu topical. Transplants help some men.
