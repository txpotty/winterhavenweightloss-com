    How ED Medication Works in Poinciana

    An erection depends on blood flowing into the penis faster than it drains out, and the enzyme PDE5 is what normally ends that process once arousal fades. Sildenafil and tadalafil block PDE5, so blood flow holds longer. Arousal still has to happen. Neither drug increases interest in sex, and both are FDA-approved for ED.

    Who ED Treatment Is For

    ED treatment is for adult men who regularly have trouble getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex, whether it started gradually over years or showed up after a stressful stretch. Roughly half of men over 40 report some degree of ED. Younger men get it too. Performance anxiety and heavy drinking are common causes in men under 40, and a low dose can help.

    Dosing and Schedule in Poinciana

    Sildenafil is usually taken 30 to 60 minutes before sex in 25, 50 or 100 mg doses, and tadalafil as needed comes in 10 or 20 mg doses that can work for up to 36 hours. A low daily tadalafil dose of 2.5 or 5 mg skips the timing entirely. One dose per day, maximum. After 4 to 6 attempts, the dose may move up or down.

    Many men notice a difference the first time, but the honest advice from most providers is to give a new medication at least 4 to 6 tries before judging it, since nerves often get in the way early. Taking sildenafil after a heavy meal is the most common reason it seems weak. Track what works. If you are on daily tadalafil, expect steady effect after roughly 5 days.
